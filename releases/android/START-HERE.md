# Welcome to ElectroLessonLab (ELL) Beta

This is a private Android test of a game about industrial electrical concepts. The app contains 47 short missions, four practice labs and Coach Click. Use it only for conceptual practice. Do not perform electrical work or connect real equipment for this test.

1. Get the APK and your tester code from the organizer. Open the APK on your Android phone and choose **Install**. Android may ask you to allow that browser/file manager to install this app; turn that permission off again afterward. Keep device protections enabled. Ask the organizer for help if installation is blocked.
2. Open **ElectroLessonLab Beta**, then tap **Beta** at the bottom. Read the recording information. Enter your assigned code, choose your group and select **Start beta recording** if you opt in. No name or account is needed. You may use the training without recording.
3. Tap **Learn**. Start with safety. Read the notes, answer the three questions and read each explanation. Use coach hints when you need them.
4. Try all four **Lab** activities. Explore the component pictures and try the component challenge. Tell the organizer which explanations you can understand and which need work.
5. Leave an unfinished mission, close the app and reopen it. Try **Resume mission**. Does it recover your work as expected?
6. Take breaks whenever needed. Keep using the same device. Do not uninstall or clear storage before sharing your results. Follow the organizer's plan if you are continuing through the full course.
7. Return to **Beta** to save feedback. Describe the mission/lab, what happened and what you expected. Include no names, employer details or workplace documents.
8. At the end, pause recording and tap **Share beta results**. Choose the contact/app agreed with your organizer, send the complete record and confirm it arrived. Opening the share sheet alone does not send it. Keep the local record until delivery is confirmed.

Your opted-in record stays on this phone unless you share it. It includes your tester code/group, recorded answers, hints, approximate foreground time and feedback. Nothing is uploaded automatically by ELL. Timing includes idle time while training is visible; background time and the Beta tab are excluded. Replays remain separate from first attempts. Scores indicate game progress, not professional qualification.

Teens should participate with the organizer's appropriate parent/guardian arrangements and supervision. Stop whenever you want. You can pause recording or delete the beta record after exporting; deleting it does not erase course scores.

Requires Android 7 or newer on an ARM phone. The APK includes the lessons and graphics and does not require Expo Go or a development server.

Beta 0.2.0-beta.1. Report installation, readability, navigation, content or sharing problems to your organizer. The organizer should test the APK on their own phone before distributing it to the group.
