# Draft request: commercial reference use and app ownership clarification

**UNSENT TEMPLATE.** Personalize privately. This request itself is not a permission grant, assignment or clearance. Have counsel adapt it to the actual ownership and employment facts. Do not send private prints or agreements unnecessarily.

To: [authorized rights-holder / employer representative]

From: [developer's legal name and, if applicable, entity]

Date: [date]

Subject: Written clarification of rights for ElectroLessonLab (ELL)

I am developing ElectroLessonLab, a mobile learning application for industrial electrical concepts. It uses lessons, quizzes, a relay coach called Click and virtual practice circuits. I intend to distribute it commercially to individual learners and employers in the United States.

The materials listed in the private Schedule A informed topic selection and explanation. The app is intended to contain independently expressed teaching and original examples, without distributing source documents, proprietary prints, identifiable machine/customer information, source figures, company marks or confidential procedures. This description is a proposed scope for your review, not a representation that all permission/ownership questions have already been resolved.

Please arrange a written response from a person authorized to address these separate matters:

1. **Rights holder and authority.** Which Schedule A items do you actually own/control? Which belong to a publisher, customer, standards organization or another party whose permission you cannot grant? Please identify any contractual/confidential restrictions separately from copyright.
2. **Reference/adaptation permission.** For material you control, may I use the specifically identified nonconfidential content as a reference to create, modify, sell, distribute and update independent teaching in ELL? Please identify precisely what expression, if any, may be adapted and the permitted purposes, territories, term, payment/attribution requirements and limits. No permission to reproduce source prints, full documents or marks is requested by default.
3. **App ownership.** Does the employer or another party assert an ownership, invention-assignment, work-made-for-hire or other claim to ELL's code, curriculum, graphics or branding because of my employment, contracts, work duties, equipment/time or source access? If so, what claim and what written release, license or assignment would be required? Permission to consult a manual does not resolve this separate issue.
4. **Confidentiality and handling.** What information must be excluded? Please identify applicable information-security and AI-service processing restrictions for reference material. Any review of earlier handling or proposed future handling should be addressed expressly with counsel rather than assumed authorized.
5. **Publication conditions.** Are there any specific approval, attribution, review, update or revocation conditions for future releases? Silence or informal encouragement will not be treated as permission. No endorsement or certification by your organization will be claimed unless separately authorized.

If the proposed commercial use is not allowed, please identify affected material so I can exclude it and determine whether a genuinely independent alternative is possible. Rewording confidential information alone will not be treated as resolving a restriction.

Thank you,

[legal name / business contact]
