US names, titles, and short phrases are generally a trademark/unfair-competition question rather than ownership of copyright in the words alone. Character artwork, text, software, screenshots, and expressive selection/arrangement present separate copyright questions. A name match does not automatically prove infringement, and a search with no exact match would not prove availability. Similar sounds, spellings, related goods/services, territories, unregistered use, and overall presentation can matter.

## Tone and coach rules

The user prefers a playful trade nickname. The working nickname Click follows that direction without treating any candidate as cleared. The coach is a compact original relay character with a copper-coil window, neutral hardhat, blue panels, and lime accents. Four generated poses support welcome, reflection, encouragement, and modest celebration. Hints are optional scripted prompts about reasoning, not live diagnostic advice or hidden answer reveals. Incorrect answers receive a thoughtful expression, not ridicule. Mastery celebrations acknowledge game performance only. No time pressure, dangerous live-work dares, invented certification, safety shortcuts, competitor imitation, or penalty for asking for help is introduced.
